Common help topics

  • Reminders are not arriving: check Daily Reminders in the app and notification permissions in your device’s Settings. Summaries include events within your chosen look-ahead window; there may be no summary on days without matching events. Open the app to refresh the schedule.
  • Next candidate (build 11): summaries are prepared for up to 30 days. If more reminders are needed, “Refresh your event reminders” asks you to reopen the app when that schedule ends. This change is prepared for the next beta and is not yet in the distributed build.
  • Widgets look out of date: open the app to refresh the widget snapshot and allow iOS time to update.
  • Beta timetable imports: if a shared Widgit link did not prefill, open the app once and check that the link is a valid Widgit Online document URL. This beta feature’s inclusion at launch is awaiting permission; HowManySleeps is not affiliated with or endorsed by Widgit.
  • Can’t add, edit or delete: check Standard User Permissions in Settings. Read Only prevents changes; Add Only allows new events but prevents editing or deleting existing ones. The superuser can apply a different permission level with the PIN.
  • Forgotten PIN: use the recovery option under Reset Superuser PIN and authenticate with your device. The PIN controls editing on this device; it does not hide event details in the app, widgets, notifications or backups.
  • An event seems to be missing: tap the up/down arrows beside + and set Show to All. Filtering does not delete events. Repeating events move on to today or their next occurrence; earlier repeats are not shown in Past. See the sort and filter guide.

Sorting and filtering your events

Tap the up/down arrows beside + on the event list. Sort by Next Date (earliest date first), Name (A to Z), or Date Added (oldest added first). You can combine a sort order with any Show filter.

Upcoming includes today and future dates. Past shows one-off events dated before today. All shows one row per saved event, not a history of every repeat. A repeating event uses today or its next occurrence, so its earlier repeats do not appear in Past.

Your Show choice is remembered on this device. Sorting returns to Next Date when the app restarts. Read the full sort and filter instructions.

iCloud sync

Check that each device uses the same Apple Account, iCloud is available for HowManySleeps, and the devices have an internet connection. Open the app on both devices and allow time for sync.

If an event remains after closing and reopening the app, it has saved locally even if a sync warning is shown. If a warning persists, contact support with the exact message and app build. Export a backup before trying recovery steps.

Backups and restoring events

Use Settings → Data → Export Backup to save a copy of your events. Backup files contain readable event details and optional timetable links, so keep them somewhere private.

Import Backup replaces your current events, including on synced devices. Export your current events first if you want to keep them. Erase All Events also removes synced copies.

Next candidate (build 11): backups use format 2 to preserve calendar dates across time zones. Older format 1 backups are rejected with an explanatory message. If you need help moving from an older beta, contact support before changing your data.

Before you contact support

  • Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and app version/build from Settings → About HowManySleeps.
  • Describe what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and any exact error message.
  • If the issue involves reminders, widgets, backups or sync, mention whether the app was recently updated.
  • Keep event details private: do not send your PIN or a full backup unless support specifically needs further information and you choose to share it.
