HowManySleeps

HowManySleeps began with Kevin’s wish to make important dates easier to understand for children and adults with special educational needs (SEN). That inspiration remains at the heart of the app, while its countdowns are there for anyone who finds them useful. Add a familiar visit, birthday, trip or regular activity and see how many sleeps remain.

Available now: Download HowManySleeps free from the UK App Store for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. No HowManySleeps account is needed, and there are no in-app purchases.

Why it exists

  • Created by an Air Traffic Management professional with 36 years of experience in clear, dependable systems
  • Inspired by the wish to make important dates easier to understand for children and adults with special educational needs (SEN)
  • Shaped by family life, with countdowns for anyone who finds familiar names, colours and dates useful

What the app supports

  • One-off and repeating countdown events
  • Sort and filter your event list
  • Colour palettes and custom colours
  • Optional event icons
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Daily reminder summaries for upcoming events
  • Optional iCloud event sync and backup export/import
  • Optional PIN editing protection with Full Editing, Add Events Only and Viewing Only modes
  • Upcoming, Past and All views, including earlier scheduled repeating dates
