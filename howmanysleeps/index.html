HowManySleeps

HowManySleeps began with Kevin’s wish to make important dates easier to understand for children and adults with special educational needs (SEN). That inspiration remains at the heart of the app, while its countdowns are there for anyone who finds them useful. Add a familiar visit, birthday, trip or regular activity and see how many sleeps remain.

Available now: Download HowManySleeps free from the UK App Store for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later. No HowManySleeps account is needed, and there are no in-app purchases.

Version 1.1 · In beta testing

What’s new in the beta

More ways to plan familiar dates, share them with family and choose who can make changes. These features are being tested through TestFlight ahead of the next App Store update.

Repeats with an end date

Choose when a repeating event should stop. Useful for a term of weekly activities or a regular visit that runs for a few months.

Events that last several days

Give a holiday or trip a first and last day. A Monday–Friday event includes all five days, with four sleeps left on Monday and “Last day” on Friday.

Share an event copy

Send an event link with an optional “Shared by” name. The recipient reviews it before adding their own copy. Event files still work.

Add and edit, without deleting

A new access mode lets someone add events and make changes while keeping deletion protected. PIN-controlled settings remain with the PIN holder.

A consistent dark appearance

The app keeps its dark appearance throughout, even when the device is set to light mode.

Already testing? When build 19 is available in TestFlight, update both devices. Choose Share Event Link and send it in Messages. On the other device, tap the link, review the event and tap Add Event.

A look inside HowManySleeps

Example events show the app on iPhone and iPad, from the next countdown to a repeating birthday.

Why it exists

  • Created by an Air Traffic Management professional with 36 years of experience in clear, dependable systems
  • Inspired by the wish to make important dates easier to understand for children and adults with special educational needs (SEN)
  • Shaped by family life, with countdowns for anyone who finds familiar names, colours and dates useful

What the app supports

  • One-off and repeating countdown events
  • Sort and filter your event list
  • Colour palettes and custom colours
  • Optional event icons
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Daily reminder summaries for upcoming events
  • Optional iCloud event sync and backup export/import
  • Optional PIN editing protection with Full Editing, Add Events Only and Viewing Only modes
  • Upcoming, Past and All views, including earlier scheduled repeating dates
