01 / Start small

One name. One date. Ready.

In HowManySleeps, a task, trip or occasion is called an event. Open the app on your iPhone or iPad and follow along. You don’t need an app account or a PIN to get started.

Step 1 of 4

  1. Tap Add Event

    On the main How Many Sleeps screen, tap the + button. If your list is empty, you can also tap Add Event in the middle of the screen.

    You’ll see the event editor. Keep the guide open while you try it in the app.

  2. Give it a name and date

    Tap Name and type something familiar, such as “Our holiday”. Tap Date and choose when it happens.

    For your first countdown, choose a future date and leave Repeats set to No Repeat.

  3. Choose a colour, if you like

    Open Colour & Icon, then tap a palette colour or use Custom Colour. You can also choose an optional icon to help recognise the event.

    A name and date are all you need. Leave these extra choices as they are if you prefer.

  4. Tap Save

    Tap Save to return to your list. Your event now shows how many sleeps remain until the date you chose.

    That’s your first countdown. To change it later, swipe left on the event and tap Edit.

02 / Make it familiar

A few options. Your way.

Find these in Add Event, or swipe left on an existing event and tap Edit. Tap Save when you’re done.

Repeating events

Choose Days, Weeks, Months or Years under Repeats, then adjust “Every” to set the interval. For a weekly activity, choose Weeks and Every 1 week. Use No Repeat for a one-off occasion.

Colours & icons

Use a recognisable colour for each kind of event. Pick from the palette or choose Custom Colour. An Icon is optional; choose None for a simpler look. Change the available palette in Settings.

A tour when you need it

The optional first-launch tour shows Add Event, repeats, a saved countdown and Settings. Choose Not now or Skip tour whenever you like. Replay it from Settings → Help & About → Show Me Around.

A simple first setup

Start with one event, a clear name and a colour you recognise. Add repeats or reminders when they’re useful to you.

Sort and filter your events

Choose a sort order to arrange the list, and a Show filter to decide which events appear. You can combine any sort order with any filter. Filtering does not delete events.

Choose a sort order
  • Next Date: earliest date first. In Past or All, the oldest dates appear first.
  • Name: A to Z, ignoring capital letters.
  • Date Added: oldest added first.
Choose which dates to show
  • Upcoming: one occurrence today or next for each saved repeating series, alongside upcoming one-off events.
  • Past: every scheduled occurrence from a repeating series’ current saved start date up to yesterday, alongside past one-off events.
  • All: those past occurrences plus the one today or next for each repeating series, alongside one-off events.

Past cards show “1 sleep ago” or “N sleeps ago”, using the number of days since that date. Next Date puts the oldest dates first.

Past repeating cards are read-only. To edit or delete the saved series, find it in Upcoming and use its Edit or Delete action, if your access level allows it.

The history is calculated from the series’ current saved start date and repeat setting. Changing either recalculates the dates shown; these are not separate attendance records. Deleting the series also removes its past dates.

The list loads 100 cards at a time. Tap Load More Events to see the next 100.

Your Show choice is remembered on this device. The sort order returns to Next Date when the app restarts.

04 / Settle in

Find the setting you need.

Tap the gear icon on the main screen to open Settings. Choose a topic below for the exact route.

Change the colour palette

Settings → Colours

In Colour Palettes, use the Palette picker to choose a set of colours. Use Add Palette to create your own. Tap Done to return to Settings, then Done again to return to your events.

Events using a palette colour follow that palette slot when you change palettes. Events with a Custom Colour keep their chosen colour.

Turn on a daily reminder

Settings → Daily Reminders

  1. Turn on Enable Daily Reminder and allow notifications if asked.
  2. Choose your Reminder Time.
  3. Set Look ahead to between 1 and 30 days.
  4. Choose whether to Include events happening today.

Reminders are daily summaries of events within your look-ahead window. They are not separate alarms for each event, and there may be no summary on days without matching events. Open the app regularly to refresh the schedule.

Summaries are prepared for up to 30 days. If more reminders are needed, a Refresh your event reminders notification asks you to reopen the app when the prepared schedule ends. Notification delivery also depends on your device settings.

If notifications were previously denied, use Open Settings in the app’s notification alert to allow them in your device settings.

Set up a PIN and choose who can change events

Settings → Editing Protection → Set a PIN

You can use Full Editing without a PIN. To limit changes on this device, choose Set a PIN and create and confirm a 4-digit PIN, then choose an editing access mode in Editing Protection.

Available editing access modes
Editing accessWhat the user can do
Full EditingAdd, edit and delete events.
Add Events OnlyAdd new events, but cannot edit or delete existing ones.
Viewing OnlyView events without changing them.

After choosing a mode, tap Save Editing Access and enter the PIN to confirm. Check the selected mode before handing over the device.

When a PIN is set, Editing Protection shows Change PIN and Turn Off PIN Protection to manage it. If you’ve forgotten the PIN, recover editing access without losing your events.

The PIN controls editing on this device. Events remain visible in the app, widgets, notifications and exported backups.

Keep a backup of your events

Settings → Backups & Your Events

Choose Save a Backup and save the file somewhere private, such as a folder in Files. It contains readable event details, but not your app settings or PIN.

Restore from a Backup replaces your current events, including on synced devices. Save a current backup first if you want to keep those events. Choose the saved file, review the warning and choose Replace Events to continue, or Cancel to leave your events unchanged. Enter your PIN if asked. Wait for Backup restored before returning to the list.

Backups use format 2 and preserve chosen calendar dates across time zones. Each file can contain up to 5,000 events and must be no larger than 5 MB. Older format 1 files are not supported; contact support before changing your data if you need help.

Delete All Events permanently removes events, including on synced devices. It is not needed for normal setup, and it does not delete backup files you saved elsewhere.

Find help and your app version

Settings → Help & About

Use Help with HowManySleeps, Privacy Policy or Email Support for help. Find the installed Version in About HowManySleeps; include it with your device model when asking for support.

A little help

If something looks different.

I can’t add, edit or delete an event

Check Settings → Editing Protection. Full Editing allows adding, editing and deleting. Add Events Only allows new events but blocks editing and deleting; Viewing Only blocks all changes. Choose a mode, tap Save Editing Access and enter the PIN to confirm. Forgotten the PIN? Follow the recovery steps below.

I’ve forgotten my PIN. Will I lose my events?

No. PIN recovery keeps all your events. It removes the PIN on this device and restores Full Editing.

Settings → Editing Protection → Change PIN → Forgot PIN?

  1. Open Settings → Editing Protection and tap Change PIN. You do not need to enter the old PIN to reach recovery.
  2. Tap Forgot PIN? below the keypad. Scroll down if you cannot see it.
  3. Verify with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Successful verification removes this device’s PIN, restores Full Editing and keeps all events.
  4. In the confirmation alert, choose Set New PIN to protect editing again, or Done to leave the PIN off.

If Set a PIN is the only PIN action shown in Editing Protection, no PIN is set on this device, so there is no PIN to recover. Choose it if you want to set one.

PIN protection controls editing on this device; it does not hide event details in the app, widgets, notifications or exported backups.

My event has disappeared from the list

Tap the up/down arrows beside + and set Show to All. Filtering does not delete events.

Earlier scheduled repeating dates appear in Past and All. Upcoming shows today or the next occurrence for each repeating series. Past repeating cards are read-only; find the series in Upcoming to edit or delete it, if your access level allows it. Read the sort and filter guide, including how history is calculated.

My events are not syncing

Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud is available for HowManySleeps, and you have an internet connection. Open the app on both devices and allow time for sync.

If an event remains after closing and reopening the app, it has saved locally even if a sync warning appears. For a persistent warning, export a backup and contact support with the exact message and app build.

Save isn’t working

Check that the event has a name and a valid date. Follow any message shown by the app. If editing is restricted, check Settings → Editing Protection.

Where do I add a widget?

Widgets are added from your iPhone or iPad’s Home Screen widget gallery. Look for HowManySleeps, choose a size and add it. They aren’t added from the app’s Settings screen.

If a widget looks out of date, open HowManySleeps and give your device time to refresh it.
