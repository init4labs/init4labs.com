HowManySleeps privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026. This policy describes the current HowManySleeps app and beta features, including optional timetable imports where present.

1. Introduction

HowManySleeps ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app processes, how it is used, and your choices.

Contact: hello@init4labs.com

2. Data we process

  • Event data you create, such as titles, calendar dates, repeat settings, colour and icon choices, and optional timetable links
  • Shared content from iOS or iPadOS share sheet imports, such as a Widgit link and page title you explicitly share to the app
  • App settings, such as filters, palette preferences and reminder settings
  • Security settings, such as whether PIN protection is enabled, permission levels, failed PIN attempts and temporary lockout information
  • The success or failure of device authentication when you choose PIN recovery. Apple handles Face ID, Touch ID or device-passcode authentication; the app does not receive your biometric data or device passcode
  • Widget snapshot data stored in the app group container so widgets can display upcoming events
  • Backup files you choose to export or import, containing readable event details and optional timetable links
  • Information you choose to send by email when asking for support

3. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules local notifications. Summaries can contain event titles and countdowns, which may be visible on your Lock Screen depending on your notification settings.

You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings or in the app’s settings.

4. iCloud and CloudKit sync

Events are stored locally on your device. When iCloud is available for the app, event data may also be synchronised between your devices using Apple CloudKit and your Apple Account. You do not need a separate HowManySleeps account.

Apple processes CloudKit data under Apple’s privacy policy and its applicable service terms. Local settings and PIN protection are managed on each device.

5. External links, support and tracking

We do not sell your personal data.

In beta builds that include Widgit Online imports, the share extension may request the page you explicitly share to find its direct document link. Opening a timetable also connects to the external website. That service receives normal web-request information, such as your IP address, and applies its own privacy policy. HowManySleeps is not affiliated with or endorsed by Widgit.

When you choose Email Support, your email provider sends the message and any attachments you include to Init4Labs. Share only the information you want support to receive.

The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

HowManySleeps does not track you across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

6. Data retention

Event data remains on your device and, where synchronised, in iCloud until you change or delete it. Deleting synced events or using Erase All Events also affects their copies on your synced devices. Removing the app does not by itself guarantee that copies in iCloud or exported backups are deleted.

Exported backup files remain wherever you saved or shared them until you delete those copies. Import Backup replaces current events, including on synced devices. Support emails remain in the support mailbox for handling your enquiry; contact us if you want to request their deletion.

7. Security

  • A salted PIN hash, permission level and retry/lockout information stored in iOS Keychain
  • iOS app sandboxing
  • App Group container isolation for share extension and widget data handoff
  • Apple-managed CloudKit security model

The superuser PIN controls editing on the device. It does not hide events in the app, widgets, notifications or exported backups. Exported backups are readable JSON files and are not encrypted by the app; keep them in a private location.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but reasonable safeguards are used.

8. Your choices

  • Edit or delete events in the app
  • Disable reminders
  • Remove PIN protection
  • Remove the app and associated local data
  • Manage iCloud availability through Apple device settings
  • Choose whether to export or share a backup, and delete saved backup copies yourself
  • Control Lock Screen notification previews in device settings
  • Choose what information to include in a support email

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The “Last updated” date on this page will be changed when updates are made.
